### Research Focus & Engineering Directions

1. **Real-Time AI & Spatial Vision Systems**
   - At **USC**, I built a real-time SLAM pipeline combining monocular visual odometry (VO) and depth estimation, achieving ~20–25 FPS on noisy indoor sequences.
   - Leveraged RANSAC-based feature matching (2500 matches, 2000 inliers) with IMU motion constraints, fusing VO with GPS in GTSAM factor graphs to reduce trajectory error from ~95 m to ~1.2 m ATE.
   - Engineered topological semantic memory for open-vocabulary scene understanding beyond fixed COCO classes.

2. **Personalization & Recommendation Infrastructure**
   - At **Tabhi**, I architected an end-to-end personalized discovery feed (retrieve → rank → re-rank) for a 188K-item catalog using hybrid Elasticsearch BM25 and vector retrieval with slot-based re-ranking.
   - Designed a recency-decayed user preference model and reduced cold-feed latency from 8s to <500 ms via three-tier caching, cache pre-warming, and geospatial index optimization.
